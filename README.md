# 🍷 VinoGram · Tlhabi 🇿🇦 & Niels 🇫🇷

An Instagram-inspired private wine tasting journal, terroir map, couple statistics battle, and AI Sommelier built with **Streamlit** and **Gemini Vision** for **Tlhabi** (South Africa 🇿🇦) and **Niels** (France 🇫🇷).

## ✨ Features
1. **📱 Wine Feed (Instagram Scroll)**:
   - Scroll through every bottle tasted with either a real photo or a dynamically generated **Luxury Estate SVG Wine Bottle** printed with the Domaine, Cuvée, Vintage, Grape, and Region.
   - Displays where you tasted it, food pairings, price (€), aroma hashtags, and individual ratings/notes from Tlhabi & Niels.
2. **➕ Central "Record Wine" Studio**:
   - **AI Label Scanner**: Take a picture of any wine bottle label with your phone camera or upload a photo—Gemini Vision automatically extracts the Domaine, Cuvée, Vintage, Grape Varieties, Country, Region, Price, and Aromas.
   - **Manual & Assisted Entry**: Log where you drank it, with what food, price, and who tasted it (`Both Tlhabi & Niels`, `Only Tlhabi`, or `Only Niels`) with separate score sliders (`0–10`) for each of you.
3. **🌍 World Wine Map**:
   - Toggle between **Vineyard Origins** (where the wines come from across South Africa, France, and the world) and **Tasting Spots** (where you drank them).
4. **📊 Stats & Couple Battle (Tlhabi 🇿🇦 vs Niels 🇫🇷)**:
   - Side-by-side palate DNA (favourite wine styles, grapes, regions, Top 3 bottles).
   - **Funny Couple Awards**: *Who drinks the most expensive wines?*, *Tougher Critic vs Wine Optimist*, *The Patriotism Index (SA vs France bias)*, and *Soulmate Bottle vs Biggest Debate*.
5. **🤖 AI Sommelier & Restaurant Menu Scanner**:
   - Editable **Free-Text Preference Boxes** for both Tlhabi and Niels.
   - Snap a photo of the **food & drinks menu at a restaurant** and get tailored picks for the best bottle to share, top pick for Tlhabi, top pick for Niels, and best value gem.
